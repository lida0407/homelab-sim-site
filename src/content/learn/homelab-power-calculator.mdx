## The short answer

> **cost per month = average watts × 730 ÷ 1,000 × price per kWh**

730 is the number of hours in an average month (8,760 ÷ 12). A lab averaging 200 W uses 146 kWh a month: about **€44 at €0.30/kWh** or **$25 at $0.17/kWh**. Use the price per kWh from your own bill, including network charges and taxes; regional averages hide large differences.

## Typical starting values

Use these only until you've measured. Real draw varies a lot with CPU, BIOS settings, disks and load.

| Device | Idle W | Load W |
|---|---|---|
| Mini PC, low-power quad-core | 8 | 25 |
| Mini PC, 6–8 cores | 12 | 50 |
| Raspberry Pi / SBC | 4 | 8 |
| NAS, 4 bays, HDDs spinning | 30 | 45 |
| 1U server, older dual-socket | 80 | 160 |
| 2U server, older dual-socket | 110 | 220 |
| Desktop tower used as a server | 35 | 120 |
| AI box with one 24 GB GPU | 60 | 400 |
| Switch, 8-port unmanaged | 4 | 5 |
| Switch, 24-port managed (without PoE load) | 20 | 25 |
| Router / firewall appliance | 12 | 18 |
| Wi-Fi access point (not on PoE) | 6 | 12 |

Example lab: one 2U server busy 4 h a day, plus an idle 4-bay NAS, two low-power mini PCs, a 24-port switch and a router, averages about 206 W. That's ≈ 151 kWh, or ≈ €45 a month at €0.30/kWh, and the 2U server alone is 62 % of it.

## Measure, don't guess

- **Whole lab:** a plug-in meter or smart plug at the strip that feeds the rack, or your UPS's output watts (if the UPS reports only a load percentage, multiply it by the rated watts).
- **Per device:** a smart plug per device for a week, or the server's management controller (`ipmitool dcmi power reading` on most rack servers).
- **Over time:** log readings to see idle versus busy hours. That's what turns the "Busy h/day" column from a guess into a number.

## Where the money goes

1. **Idle draw, around the clock.** A server idling at 110 W costs about €290 a year at €0.30/kWh even if it never does anything.
2. **Old enterprise servers.** Dual-socket boxes with many fans and drives idle high. Replacing one with a mini PC often pays for itself in electricity within a year or two; work it out with the payback calculator in the Proxmox power guide.
3. **Spinning disks.** Each 3.5" drive spinning idle adds a few watts; a 12-bay array adds up.
4. **The GPU you forgot.** An AI box that idles at 60 W costs more per month than the four mini PCs next to it.
