summary: "Import an Excel or CSV of your gear and get a 3D model of your rack with Power, Heat, Network, Cables, Compute and AI views, diagrams and a Fail what-if."
---

- **Spreadsheet in, 3D rack out.** Fill in the template (only Devices is required: rack, U, model) or one CSV per sheet. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.
- **An import report you can act on.** Rows imported, rows skipped with the reason, and checks such as two devices in one U, a port used twice, 10G on a 1G port, both power supplies on one feed, or a power strip over 80 %.
- **Seven views.** Overview, Power, Heat, Network, Cables, Compute and AI, on one or two racks, 10-inch racks included.
- **Power and network diagrams.** A one-line power diagram (wall → UPS → strip → device, load on each node) and a network topology. Click any node and press **Fail** to see what goes dark.
- **Cables at the back of the rack.** Every run drawn, filtered by type and traced end to end through patch panels.
- **A demo lab to explore.** The "Basement Lab" comes with 30 days of simulated history to scrub through.
- **Made for posting.** Download a share image and a rack-elevation PNG.
